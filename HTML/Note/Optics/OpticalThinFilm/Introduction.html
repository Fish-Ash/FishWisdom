复折射率

$$N = c/v = n - ik$$
$$\frac{c^2}{v^2} = \frac{\epsilon\mu}{\epsilon_0\mu_0} - i\frac{\mu\sigma}{\omega\epsilon_0\mu_0}$$

\(c/v\)为介质固有的一个无量纲参数，将其记为N.

$$N^2 = \epsilon_r\mu_r - i\frac{\mu_r\sigma}{\omega\epsilon_0}$$

\(N\)有两个可能的解，但由于物理上的原因，应取\(n\)为正值的解.

  • \(n\)：折射率的实部（或简单称为介质的折射率，因为在理想的介质材料中N为实数）
  • \(k\)：消光系数
$$\begin{align} N^2 &= (n-ik)^2\\ &= n^2 - k^2 - 2nki\\ &= \epsilon_r\mu_r - i\frac{\mu_r\sigma}{\omega\epsilon_0} \end{align}$$ $$\begin{align}\therefore\quad &n^2-k^2 = \epsilon_r\mu_r\\ &2nk = \frac{\mu_r\sigma}{\omega\epsilon_0} \end{align}$$

通常有：

$$\mu_r\approx 1$$ $$\begin{align}\therefore\quad &n^2-k^2 = \epsilon_r\\ &2nk = \frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon_0} \end{align}$$

单色平面波方程

沿方向\(r\)传播的单色平面波 $$\begin{align} \boldsymbol{E} &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\left[i\omega\left(t-\frac{r}{v}\right)\right]\\ &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\left[i\left(\omega t - \frac{2\pi Nr}{\lambda}\right)\right] \end{align}$$

将\(N = n-ik\)代入得：

$$\boldsymbol{E} = \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\left(-\frac{2\pi kr}{\lambda}\right)\exp\left[i\left(\omega t - \frac{2\pi nr}{\lambda}\right)\right]$$
$$\begin{align} \boldsymbol{E} &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\left[i\left(\omega t - \frac{2\pi Nr}{\lambda}\right)\right]\\ &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\left[i\omega t - i\frac{2\pi(n-ik)r}{\lambda}\right]\\ &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\left[i\left(\omega t - \frac{2\pi nr}{\lambda}\right) - \frac{2\pi kr}{\lambda}\right]\\ &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\left(-\frac{2\pi kr}{\lambda}\right)\exp\left[i\left(\omega t - \frac{2\pi nr}{\lambda}\right)\right] \end{align}$$

上式说明：电磁波在导电介质（\(\sigma\neq 0\)，因此\(k\neq 0\)）中是一个衰减波，消光系数\(k\)是介质吸收电磁能量的度量.

沿给定方向余弦\((\alpha,\beta,\gamma)\)传播的单色平面波 $$\begin{align} \boldsymbol{E} &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\{i[\omega t - \frac{2\pi N}{\lambda}(\alpha x + \beta y + \gamma z)]\}\\ &= \boldsymbol{E}_0\exp\{i[\omega t - \frac{2\pi N}{\lambda}\boldsymbol{S_0}\cdot \boldsymbol{r}]\} \end{align}$$ 其中，\(\boldsymbol{S_0} = (\alpha, \beta, \gamma)\)为方向余弦，\(\boldsymbol{r} = (x, y, z)\)为坐标矢径。
$$ \begin{align} &\begin{cases} \nabla\times\boldsymbol{H} = \boldsymbol{j} + \frac{\partial \boldsymbol{D}}{\partial t}\\ \frac{\partial \boldsymbol{E}}{\partial t} = i\omega\boldsymbol{E}\\ \boldsymbol{D} = \epsilon \boldsymbol{E}\\ \boldsymbol{j} = \sigma \boldsymbol{E} \end{cases} \Rightarrow \nabla\times\boldsymbol{H} = (\sigma + i\omega\epsilon)\boldsymbol{E}\\ &\because N^2 = (\epsilon\mu - i\frac{\sigma\mu}{\omega})/\epsilon_0\mu_0\\ &\therefore \sigma+i\omega\epsilon = i\frac{\omega\epsilon_0\mu_0N^2}{\mu} = i\frac{\omega N^2}{\mu c^2}\\ &\therefore \nabla \times \boldsymbol{H} = \frac{\omega N^2}{\mu c^2}\boldsymbol{E} \end{align} $$

介质的光学导纳

自由空间导纳 $$y_0 = \sqrt{\epsilon_0/\mu_0}$$

SI中，其值为1/377西门子.

磁场强度\(\boldsymbol{H}\)与电场强度\(\boldsymbol{E}\)之比。

$$Y = \frac{|\boldsymbol{H}|}{|\boldsymbol{S}_0 \times \boldsymbol{E}|} = \frac{|\boldsymbol{H}|}{|\boldsymbol{E}|} = \frac{N\sqrt{\epsilon_0/\mu_0}}{\mu_r}$$ \(\boldsymbol{S_0}\)：平面波传播方向的单位矢量。

在光波段，\(\mu_r\rightarrow 1\)，有

$$Y = N\cdot y_0$$

若以自由空间导纳\(y_0\)为单位，光学导纳也可表示为：

$$Y = N$$
反射率\(R\)

反射光强度与入射光强度之比。

透射率\(T\)

透射光强度与入射光强度之比。

有效导纳\(\eta\)

磁场强度的切向分量与电场方向的切向分量之比。

$$\eta = \boldsymbol{H}_t^+/(\boldsymbol{S}_0\times \boldsymbol{E}_t^+) = -\boldsymbol{H}_t^-/(\boldsymbol{S}_0\times \boldsymbol{E}_t^-) $$

对于TM波，引入边界条件，有：

$$\boldsymbol{H}_t = \boldsymbol{H}$$ $$\boldsymbol{E}_t = \boldsymbol{E}\cos\theta$$ 其中，\(\theta\)为\(\boldsymbol{E}\)与界面的夹角。 $$\boldsymbol{H}_t = \boldsymbol{H} = N(\boldsymbol{S}_0\times \boldsymbol{E}) = N(\boldsymbol{r}_0\times\boldsymbol{E}_t/\cos\theta) = \frac{N}{\cos\theta}(\boldsymbol{r}_0\times\boldsymbol{E}_t)$$ $$\eta_p = \boldsymbol{H}_t/(\boldsymbol{S}_0\times \boldsymbol{E}_t) = \frac{N}{\cos\theta}$$

对于TE波，引入边界条件，有：

$$ \boldsymbol{E}_t = \boldsymbol{E}\\ \boldsymbol{H}_t = \boldsymbol{H}\cos\theta $$ 其中，\(\theta\)为\(\boldsymbol{H}\)与界面的夹角。 $$\boldsymbol{S}_0\times \boldsymbol{E}_t = \boldsymbol{S}_0\times\boldsymbol{E} = \boldsymbol{H}/N = \frac{1}{N\cos\theta}\boldsymbol{H}$$ $$\eta_p = \frac{\boldsymbol{H}}{(\boldsymbol{S}_0\times\boldsymbol{E})} = N\cos\theta$$

单层介质薄膜的反射率

单层膜的反射系数：

$$r = \frac{\eta_0 - Y}{\eta_0 + Y}$$ $$R = r^2 = (\frac{\eta_0 - Y}{\eta_0 + Y})^2$$

设\(\boldsymbol{E_{1t}}\)为在界面\(t\)上的\(\boldsymbol{E_1}\)。

$$\begin{cases} E_0 = E_0^+ + E_0^- = E_{11}^+ + E_{11}^-\\ H_0 = H_0^+ + H_0^- = \eta_1E_{11}^+ - \eta_1E_{11}^- \end{cases}$$ $$\because\begin{cases} E_{12}^+ = e^{-i\delta_1}E_{11}^+\\ E_{12}^- = e^{i\delta_1}E_{11}^- \end{cases}$$ $$\therefore\begin{cases} E_0 = e^{i\delta_1}E_{12}^+ + e^{-i\delta_1}E_{12}^-\\ H_0 = \eta_1e^{i\delta_1}E_{12}^+ + \eta_1e^{-i\delta_1}E_{12}^- \end{cases}$$ $$\begin{bmatrix} E_0\\ H_0 \end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix} e^{i\delta_1} & e^{-i\delta_1}\\ \eta_1e^{i\delta_1} & -\eta_1e^{-i\delta_1} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} E_{12}^+\\ E_{12}^- \end{bmatrix} $$ $$\begin{align} \begin{bmatrix}E_0\\H_0\end{bmatrix} &=\begin{bmatrix} e^{i\delta_1} & e^{-i\delta_1}\\ \eta_1e^{i\delta_1} & -\eta_1e^{-i\delta_1} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{2} & \frac{1}{2\eta_1}\\ \frac{1}{2} & -\frac{1}{2\eta_1} \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}E_2\\H_2\end{bmatrix}\\ &=\begin{bmatrix} \cos\delta_1 & \frac{i}{\eta_1}\sin\delta_1\\ i\eta_1\sin\delta_1 & \cos\delta_1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}E_2\\H_2\end{bmatrix} \end{align}$$ $$\because \begin{cases} H_0 = YE_0\\ H_2 = \eta_2E_2 \end{cases}$$ $$\therefore E_0\begin{bmatrix}1\\Y\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix} \cos\delta_1 & \frac{i}{\eta_1}\sin\delta_1\\ i\eta_1\sin\delta_1 & \cos\delta_1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}1\\\eta_2\end{bmatrix}E_2$$ 令 $$\begin{bmatrix}1\\Y\end{bmatrix}= \begin{bmatrix}B\\C\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\delta_1 & \frac{i}{\eta_1}\sin\delta_1\\ i\eta_1\sin\delta_1 & \cos\delta_1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}1\\\eta_2\end{bmatrix}$$ $$\therefore Y = C/B = \frac{\eta_2\cos\delta_1+i\eta_1\cos\delta_1}{\cos\delta_1+i(\eta_2/\eta_1)\sin\delta_1}$$ 薄膜的特征矩阵 $$\begin{bmatrix} \cos\delta_1 & \frac{i}{\eta_1}\sin\delta_1\\ i\eta_1\sin\delta_1 & \cos\delta_1 \end{bmatrix}$$

当薄膜的有效光学厚度为\(1/4\)波长的整数倍时，即

$$nd\cos\theta = m\frac{\lambda_0}{4}$$

其相位厚度为\(\frac\pi2\)的整数倍，即

$$\delta = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}nd\cos\theta = \frac{m}{2}\pi$$

\(m\)为奇数时：\(\begin{cases}\cos\delta_1 = 0\\\sin\delta_1 = 1~or~-1\end{cases}\)

$$\begin{align} \therefore \begin{bmatrix}1\\Y\end{bmatrix} &= \begin{bmatrix}0&\pm i/\eta_1\\\pm i\eta_1&0\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}1\\\eta_s\end{bmatrix}\\ &= \begin{bmatrix}\pm i\frac{\eta_s}{\eta_1}\\\pm i\eta_1\end{bmatrix} \end{align}$$ $$\therefore Y = \eta_1^2/\eta_s$$ （四分之一波长法则） $$R = (\frac{\eta_0 - \eta_1^2/\eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_1^2/\eta_s})^2$$

\(m\)为偶数时：\(\begin{cases}\cos\delta_1 = 1~or~-1\\\sin\delta_1 = 0\end{cases}\)

$$\begin{align} \therefore \begin{bmatrix}1\\Y\end{bmatrix} &= \begin{bmatrix}\pm 1&0\\0&\pm 1\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}1\\\eta_s\end{bmatrix}\\ &= \begin{bmatrix}\pm1\\\pm\eta_s\end{bmatrix} \end{align}$$ $$\therefore Y = \eta_s$$ $$R = (\frac{\eta_0 - \eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_s})^2$$

此时，对于参考波长\(\lambda_0\)，该膜层对膜系的反射或透射特性无任何影响，故称为虚设层。

在其他波长上，薄膜的特征矩阵不再是单位矩阵，对膜系的特征是有影响的，故半波长厚度的虚设层常用于平滑膜系的分光特性。

当\(nd\cos\theta\)为\(\frac{\lambda}{4}\)的整数倍时，反射率将达到极值。

  • \(\eta_1 > \eta_s\)时，\(\frac{\lambda}{4}\)奇数倍波长\(R\)达到极大值，偶数倍波长\(R\)达到极小值。
  • \(\eta_1 = \eta_s\)时，\(R\)恒为定值。
  • \(\eta_1 < \eta_s\)时，\(\frac{\lambda}{4}\)奇数倍波长\(R\)达到最小值，偶数倍波长\(R\)达到极大值。
$$ (\frac{\eta_0 - \eta_1^2/\eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_1^2/\eta_s})^2 - (\frac{\eta_0 - \eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_s})^2 = (\frac{\eta_0 - \eta_1^2/\eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_1^2/\eta_s} + \frac{\eta_0 - \eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_s})(\frac{\eta_0 - \eta_1^2/\eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_1^2/\eta_s} - \frac{\eta_0 - \eta_s}{\eta_0 + \eta_s})\\ \because (\eta_0+\eta_1^2/\eta_s)(\eta_0+\eta_s) > 0\\ \begin{align} \therefore &[(\eta_0 - \eta_1^2/\eta_s)(\eta_0 + \eta_s) + (\eta_0 + \eta_1^2/\eta_s)(\eta_0 - \eta_s)][(\eta_0 - \eta_1^2/\eta_s)(\eta_0 + \eta_s) - (\eta_0 + \eta_1^2/\eta_s)(\eta_0 - \eta_s)]\\ &=4(\eta_0^2 - \eta_1^2)(\eta_0\eta_s - \eta_0\eta_1^2/\eta_s) \end{align}\\ \because \eta_0 < \eta_1\\ R_1 > R_2:\eta_0\eta_s - \eta_0\eta_1^2/\eta_s < 0\Rightarrow \eta_1 > \eta_s\\ R_1 < R_2:\eta_0\eta_s - \eta_0\eta_1^2/\eta_s > 0\Rightarrow \eta_1 < \eta_s $$ 速写符号
