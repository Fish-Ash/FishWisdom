波函数

  • 含时Schrödinger方程

一个动力学体系的状态可以用一个归一化的波函数\(\Psi\)描述.

求解Schrödinger方程可以得到粒子的波函数\(\Psi(x,t)\).

给定适当的初始条件，Schrödinger方程可以决定以后所有时刻的波函数.

含时Schrödinger方程 $$i\hbar\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2\Psi}{\partial x^2}+V\Psi$$
  • \(\hbar = \frac{h}{2\pi} = 1.054572\times 10^{-34}J\cdot s\)
Born定则

在\(t\)时刻发现粒子在\(x\)处的概率为\(|\Psi(x,t)|^2\).

[推论]

$$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 1$$

Schrödinger方程具有自动保持波函数归一化的性质.

若\(\Psi(x,0)\)是归一化的，则\(\Psi(x,t)\)恒为归一化的，即：

$$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,0)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 1 \Rightarrow \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 1$$

要证：

$$\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,0)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 1 \Rightarrow \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 1$$

即证：

\(\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x\)是一个不随时间变化的常数.

即：

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 0$$ $$\begin{align} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x\\ &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial}{\partial t}(\Psi\cdot\Psi^*)\mathrm{d}x\\ &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t}\Psi^* + \frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial t}\Psi\mathrm{d}x \end{align}$$

由Schrödinger方程：

$$i\hbar \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t} = -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2 \Psi}{\partial x^2} + V\Psi$$

得：

$$\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t} = \frac{i\hbar}{2m}\frac{\partial^2 \Psi}{\partial x^2} - \frac{i}{h}V\Psi$$ $$\frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial t} = -\frac{i\hbar}{2m}\frac{\partial^2 \Psi^*}{\partial x^2} + \frac{i}{h}V\Psi^*$$ $$\therefore \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} |\Psi(x,t)|^2 \mathrm{d}x = 0$$ $$\therefore \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,0)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 1 \Rightarrow \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x = 1$$

算符

量子力学中，期望不是对同一个体系重复测量的平均值，而是对含有相同体系的一个系综中不同体系的重复测量的平均值.

\(t\)时刻粒子位置的期望\(\left\lt x\right\gt\) $$\left\lt x\right \gt = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}x|\Psi(x,t)|^2\mathrm{d}x$$ 粒子速度的期望\(\left\lt v\right\gt\)
$$\left\lt v\right\gt = \frac{\mathrm{d}\left\lt x\right\gt}{\mathrm{d}t} = -\frac{i\hbar}{m}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x}\Psi^*\mathrm{d}x$$
$$\begin{align} \left\lt v\right\gt &= \frac{\mathrm{d}\left\lt x\right\gt}{\mathrm{d}t}\\ &= \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}x|\Psi|^2\mathrm{d}x\\ &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}x\frac{\partial}{\partial t}(\Psi\cdot\Psi^*)\mathrm{d}x\\ &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}x(\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t}\Psi^* + \frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial t}\Psi)\mathrm{d}x \end{align}$$ $$\because\begin{cases} \frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t} = \frac{i\hbar}{2m}\frac{\partial^2 \Psi}{\partial x^2} - \frac{i}{\hbar}V\Psi\\ \frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial t} = -\frac{i\hbar}{2m}\frac{\partial^2 \Psi^*}{\partial x^2} + \frac{i}{\hbar}V\Psi^* \end{cases}$$ $$\begin{align}\therefore \left\lt v\right\gt &= \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}x\cdot\frac{i\hbar}{2m}(\frac{\partial^2 \Psi}{\partial x^2}\Psi^* - \frac{\partial^2 \Psi^*}{\partial x^2}\Psi)\mathrm{d}x\\ &= \frac{i\hbar}{2m}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}x\cdot\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x}\Psi^* - \frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial x}\Psi)\mathrm{d}x \end{align}$$

由分部积分法：

$$\left\lt v \right\gt = \frac{i\hbar}{2m}\left[x(\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x}\Psi^* - \frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial x}\Psi)\Big|_{-\infty}^{+\infty} - \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x}\Psi^* - \frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial x}\Psi\mathrm{d}x\right]$$ $$\because \lim\limits_{x\rightarrow\infty}\Psi(x,t) = 0$$ $$\therefore \left\lt v\right\gt = -\frac{i\hbar}{2m}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x}\Psi^* - \frac{\partial \Psi^*}{\partial x}\Psi\mathrm{d}x$$

由分部积分法：

$$\begin{align} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial\Psi^*}{\partial x}\Psi\mathrm{d}x &= \Psi\Psi^*\Big|_{-\infty}^{+\infty} - \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t}\Psi^*\mathrm{d}x\\ &= -\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t}\Psi^*\mathrm{d}x \end{align}$$ $$\therefore \left\lt v\right\gt = \frac{-i\hbar}{m}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial t}\Psi^*\mathrm{d}x$$
粒子动量的期望\(\left\lt p\right\gt\) $$\left\lt p\right\gt = m\left\lt v\right\gt = -i\hbar\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\frac{\partial \Psi}{\partial x}\Psi^*\mathrm{d}x$$
公式 算符 物理意义
$$\left\lt x\right\gt = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \Psi^*\cdot (x)\cdot \Psi\mathrm{d}x$$ $$x$$ 位置
$$\left\lt p\right\gt = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \Psi^* \cdot (\frac{\hbar}{i}\frac{\partial}{\partial x}) \cdot \Psi\mathrm{d}x$$ $$\frac{\hbar}{i}\frac{\partial}{\partial x}$$ 动量
de Broglie公式 $$p = \frac{h}{\lambda} = \frac{2\pi\hbar}{\lambda}$$

给出了粒子动量与\(\Psi\)波长的联系.

Heisenberg不确定原理

粒子位置确定得越精确，其动量就越不精确.

$$\sigma_x\sigma_p \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
  • \(\sigma_x\)：\(x\)的标准差
  • \(\sigma_p\)：\(p\)的标准差
