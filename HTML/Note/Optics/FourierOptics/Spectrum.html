场的频谱

任意时变场\(\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t)\)的频谱\(\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega)\)定义为：

$$\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t)e^{i\omega t}\mathrm{d}t$$

为了保证\(\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t)\)为实值场，有：

$$\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, -\omega) = \hat{\boldsymbol{E}}^*(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega)$$
$$\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, -\omega) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t)e^{-i\omega t}\mathrm{d}t$$ $$\begin{align} \hat{\boldsymbol{E}}^*(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega) &= \left[\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t)e^{i\omega t}\mathrm{d}t\right]\\ &= \frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\boldsymbol{E}^*(\boldsymbol{r}, t)e^{-i\omega t}\mathrm{d}t \end{align}$$ $$\because\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, -\omega) = \hat{\boldsymbol{E}}^*(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega)$$ $$\therefore\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t) = \boldsymbol{E}^*(\boldsymbol{r}, t)$$

即\(\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t)\)为实值场.

对含时Maxwell方程组进行Fourier变换，可得：

$$\nabla\times\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega) = i\omega\hat{\boldsymbol{B}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega)$$ $$\nabla\times\hat{\boldsymbol{H}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega) = -i\omega\hat{\boldsymbol{D}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega) + \hat{\boldsymbol{j}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega)$$ $$\nabla\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol{D}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega) = \hat{\rho}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega)$$ $$\nabla\cdot\hat{\boldsymbol{B}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega) = 0$$

一旦求得\(\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, t)\)，时变场可以通过Fourier逆变换求得：

$$\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}, t) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\hat{\boldsymbol{E}}(\boldsymbol{r}, \omega)e^{-i\omega t}\mathrm{d}\omega$$

因此，非谐电磁场的时间依赖关系可以进行傅里叶变换，并且每一个频谱分量都可以作为单色场分别处理。一般的时间依赖关系则通过逆变换恢复.
