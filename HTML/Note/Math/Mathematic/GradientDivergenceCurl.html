梯度、散度、旋度

\(\nabla\)算子

$$\nabla = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\mathbf{x} + \frac{\partial y}{\partial y}\mathbf{y} + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\mathbf{z}$$

梯度

$$\begin{align} \mathrm{d}F &= \left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}\right)\mathrm{d}x + \left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial y}\right)\mathrm{d}y + \left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial z}\right)\mathrm{d}z\\ &= \left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}\mathbf{x} + \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}\mathbf{y} + \frac{\partial F}{\partial z}\mathbf{z}\right)\cdot(\mathrm{d}x\mathbf{x} + \mathrm{d}y\mathbf{y} + \mathrm{d}z\mathbf{z})\\ &= \nabla F\cdot \mathrm{d}\mathbf{l} \end{align}$$

其中：

$$\nabla F = \left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}\mathbf{x} + \frac{\partial F}{\partial y}\mathbf{y} + \frac{\partial F}{\partial z}\mathbf{z}\right)$$

称为F的梯度.

散度

$$\begin{align} \nabla\cdot \mathbf{v} &= \left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\mathbf{x} + \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\mathbf{y} + \frac{\partial}{\partial z}\mathbf{z}\right)\cdot (v_x\mathbf{x} + v_y\mathbf{y} + v_z\mathbf{z})\\ &= \frac{\partial v_x}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial v_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial v_z}{\partial z} \end{align}$$

旋度

$$\begin{align} \nabla\times\mathbf{v} &= \left|\begin{matrix}\mathbf{x}&\mathbf{y}&\mathbf{z}\\\partial/\partial x& \partial/\partial y&\partial/\partial z\\v_x&v_y&v_z\end{matrix}\right|\\ &= \left(\frac{\partial v_z}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial v_y}{\partial v_z}\right)\mathbf{x} + \left(\frac{\partial v_x}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial v_z}{\partial v_x}\right)\mathbf{y} + \left(\frac{\partial v_y}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial v_x}{\partial y}\right)\mathbf{z} \end{align}$$
$$\nabla\times \frac{\partial \mathbf{Y}}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}(\nabla\times\mathbf{Y})$$
