逆矩阵

概念

逆矩阵

设\(\boldsymbol{A}\)是\(n\)阶方阵，若存在一个\(n\)阶方阵\(\boldsymbol{B}\)，使得\(\boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{B} = \boldsymbol{B}\boldsymbol{A} = \boldsymbol{E}\)，则称矩阵\(\boldsymbol{A}\)为可逆矩阵，矩阵\(\boldsymbol{B}\)称为\(\boldsymbol{A}\)的逆矩阵，记为\(\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}\).

[注1]逆矩阵运算仅限于方阵.

[注2]矩阵\(\boldsymbol{A}\)的逆矩阵唯一.

逆矩阵存在的条件

伴随矩阵 $$\boldsymbol{A}^* = \left(\begin{matrix} A_{11} & A_{21} & \cdots & A_{n1}\\ A_{12} & A_{22} & \cdots & A_{n2}\\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots\\ A_{13} & A_{23} & \cdots & A_{33} \end{matrix}\right)$$

其中\(A_{ij}\)为\(|\boldsymbol{A}|\)中元素\(a_{ij}\)的代数余子式.

$$\boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{A^*} = \boldsymbol{A^*}\boldsymbol{A} = |\boldsymbol{A}|\boldsymbol{E}$$
伴随矩阵的变换
$$\boldsymbol{A}^* \rightarrow \boldsymbol{A}^{-1}$$ $$\boldsymbol{A}^* = |\boldsymbol{A}|\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}$$
$$\boldsymbol{A}^*\rightarrow\boldsymbol{A}$$ $$\boldsymbol{A} = |\boldsymbol{A}|(\boldsymbol{A}^*)^{-1}$$
$$|\boldsymbol{A}^*|\rightarrow |\boldsymbol{A}|$$ $$|\boldsymbol{A}^*| = |\boldsymbol{A}|^{n-1}$$
$$|\boldsymbol{A}^*| = ||\boldsymbol{A}|\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}| = |\boldsymbol{A}|^n\frac{1}{|\boldsymbol{A}|}$$
充分必要条件

\(n\)阶方阵\(\boldsymbol{A}\)可逆\(\Leftrightarrow|\boldsymbol{A}|\neq 0\)

$$\boldsymbol{A}^{-1} = \frac{\boldsymbol{A}^*}{|\boldsymbol{A}|}$$

[注]\(\boldsymbol{A} = (a)\Rightarrow \boldsymbol{A}^{-1} = (\frac{1}{a})\)

\(|\boldsymbol{A}| \neq 0\)，\(\boldsymbol{A}\)为可逆矩阵，也称为非奇异方阵.

\(|\boldsymbol{A}| = 0\)，\(\boldsymbol{A}\)为不可逆矩阵，也称为奇异方阵.

判断矩阵可逆的方法

直接法

\(|\boldsymbol{A}| \neq 0 \Leftrightarrow \boldsymbol{A}\)可逆

\(|\boldsymbol{A}| = 0 \Leftrightarrow \boldsymbol{A}\)不可逆

逆矩阵的性质

\(\boldsymbol{A}\)可逆\(\Rightarrow\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}\)可逆，且\((\boldsymbol{A}^{-1})^{-1} = \boldsymbol{A}\).

\(\boldsymbol{A}\)可逆\(\Rightarrow\boldsymbol{A}^T\)可逆，且\((\boldsymbol{A}^T)^{-1} = (\boldsymbol{A}^{-1})^{T}\).

\(\boldsymbol{A}\)可逆，且\(k\neq 0\)\(\Rightarrow k\boldsymbol{A}\)可逆，且\((k\boldsymbol{A})^{-1} = \frac{\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}}{k}\).

\(\boldsymbol{A}\)可逆\(\Rightarrow |\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}| = |\boldsymbol{A}|^{-1}\).

\(\boldsymbol{A}_1, \boldsymbol{A}_2, \cdots \boldsymbol{A}_n\)均可逆\(\Rightarrow \boldsymbol{A}_1\boldsymbol{A}_2\cdots\boldsymbol{A}_n\)可逆，且\((\boldsymbol{A}_1\boldsymbol{A}_2\cdots\boldsymbol{A}_n)^{-1} = \boldsymbol{A}_n^{-1}\cdots\boldsymbol{A}_2^{-1}\boldsymbol{A}_1^{-1}\).

一阶方阵求逆

$$(a)^{-1} = (\frac{1}{a}) = \frac{1}{a}$$

二阶方阵求逆

$$\boldsymbol{A}^{-1} = \left(\begin{matrix}a&b\\c&d\end{matrix}\right)^{-1} = \frac{1}{|{\boldsymbol{A}}|}\left(\begin{matrix}d&-b\\-c&a\end{matrix}\right)$$

分块矩阵求逆

$$\left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{A}&\boldsymbol{O} \\ \boldsymbol{O}&\boldsymbol{B}\end{matrix}\right)^{-1} = \left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}&\boldsymbol{O} \\ \boldsymbol{O}&\boldsymbol{B}^{-1}\end{matrix}\right)$$ $$\left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{O}&\boldsymbol{A} \\ \boldsymbol{B}&\boldsymbol{O}\end{matrix}\right)^{-1} = \left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{O}&\boldsymbol{B}^{-1} \\ \boldsymbol{A}^{-1}&\boldsymbol{O}\end{matrix}\right)$$ $$\left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{A}&\boldsymbol{O} \\ \boldsymbol{C}&\boldsymbol{B}\end{matrix}\right)^{-1} = \left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}&\boldsymbol{O} \\ -\boldsymbol{B}^{-1}\boldsymbol{C}\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}&\boldsymbol{B}^{-1}\end{matrix}\right)$$ $$\left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{A}&\boldsymbol{C} \\ \boldsymbol{O}&\boldsymbol{B}\end{matrix}\right)^{-1} = \left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}&-\boldsymbol{A}^{-1}\boldsymbol{C}\boldsymbol{B}^{-1} \\ \boldsymbol{O}&\boldsymbol{B}^{-1}\end{matrix}\right)$$

初等矩阵求逆

$$\boldsymbol{E}(i,j)^{-1} = \boldsymbol{E}(i,j)$$ $$\boldsymbol{E}(i(k))^{-1} = \boldsymbol{E}(i(\frac{1}{k}))$$ $$\boldsymbol{E}(i,j(k))^{-1} = \boldsymbol{E}(i,j(-k))$$
