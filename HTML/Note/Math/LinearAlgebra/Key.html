题型

求\(\boldsymbol{A}^n\)

题目条件：\(\boldsymbol{A} = \boldsymbol{\alpha}\boldsymbol{\beta}\)

思路：\(\boldsymbol{A}^n = (\boldsymbol{\alpha}\boldsymbol{\beta})^n = \boldsymbol{\alpha}(\boldsymbol{\beta}\boldsymbol{\alpha})^{n-1}\boldsymbol{\beta}\)

题目条件：\(\left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{X_1}\\&\boldsymbol{X_2}\\&&\ddots\\&&&\boldsymbol{X}_t\end{matrix}\right)\)

思路：\(\boldsymbol{A}^n = \left(\begin{matrix}\boldsymbol{X_1}^n\\&\boldsymbol{X_2}^n\\&&\ddots\\&&&\boldsymbol{X}_t^n\end{matrix}\right)\)

已知矩阵方程\(f(\boldsymbol{A})\)，求\(g(\boldsymbol{A})\)的逆矩阵

思路：将\(f(\boldsymbol{A})\)化为\(g(\boldsymbol{A})\phi(\boldsymbol{A}) = \boldsymbol{E}\)的形式，\(\phi(\boldsymbol{A}) = g^{-1}(\boldsymbol{A})\)

要点：\(\boldsymbol{A} = \boldsymbol{A}\boldsymbol{E}\)

已知\(R\)，求未知数\(k\)

思路：通过基本行/列变换，化简矩阵，发现有\(t\)行/列无法依靠不含参数列继续化简.

此时参数的值影响这些列能否被化简为0.
