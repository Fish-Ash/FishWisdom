向量数乘

运算规律
结合律 $$\lambda(\mu \boldsymbol{a}) = \mu(\lambda \boldsymbol{a}) = (\lambda\mu)\boldsymbol{a}$$
分配律 $$(\lambda + \mu)\boldsymbol{a} = \lambda\boldsymbol{a} + \mu\boldsymbol{a}$$
$$\lambda(\boldsymbol{a} + \boldsymbol{b}) = \lambda \boldsymbol{a} + \lambda \boldsymbol{b}$$

向量点积

向量的数量积（点乘）
几何表示 $$\boldsymbol{a}\cdot\boldsymbol{b} = |\boldsymbol{a}|\cdot|\boldsymbol{b}|\cdot\cos\lt\boldsymbol{a}, \boldsymbol{b}\gt$$
代数表示 $$\boldsymbol{a}\cdot \boldsymbol{b} = a_xb_x + a_yb_y + a_zb_z$$
运算规律 交换律
结合律
分配律
几何应用 向量的模：\(|\boldsymbol{a}| = \sqrt{a^2}\)
向量夹角：\(\cos \lt\boldsymbol{a},\boldsymbol{b}\gt \frac{\boldsymbol{a}\cdot\boldsymbol{b}}{|\boldsymbol{a}|\cdot|\boldsymbol{b}|}\)
$$\boldsymbol{a}\perp\boldsymbol{b} \Rightarrow \boldsymbol{a}\cdot \boldsymbol{b} = 0$$

向量叉积

向量的向量积（叉乘）
几何表示 $$\boldsymbol{a} \times \boldsymbol{b}$$

大小：\(|\boldsymbol{a}|\cdot|\boldsymbol{b}|\cdot\sin\lt\boldsymbol{a}, \boldsymbol{b}\gt\)

方向：垂直于\(\boldsymbol{a}\)与\(\boldsymbol{b}\)，满足右手法则.
代数表示 $$\boldsymbol{a}\times \boldsymbol{b} =\left |\begin{matrix}i & j & k\\a_x & a_y & a_z\\b_x & b_y & b_z\end{matrix}\right |$$
运算规律 反交换律：\(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b} = -\boldsymbol{b}\times \boldsymbol{a}\)
分配律
几何应用 同时垂直于\(\boldsymbol{a}\)和\(\boldsymbol{b}\)的向量：\(\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}\)
以\(\boldsymbol{a}\)与\(\boldsymbol{b}\)为边的平行四边形面积：\(S = |\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}| = |\boldsymbol{a}|\cdot|\boldsymbol{b}|\cdot\sin\lt \boldsymbol{a}, \boldsymbol{b}\gt\)
$$\boldsymbol{a} // \boldsymbol{b} \Rightarrow \boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b} = 0$$

向量混合积

混合积
几何表示 $$[a,b,c] = (a\times b)\cdot c$$
代数表示 $$[a,b,c] = \left |\begin{matrix}a_x & a_y & a_z\\b_x & b_y & b_z\\c_x & c_y & c_z\end{matrix}\right |$$
运算规律 交换变号：\([a,b,c] = -[b,a,c] = -[a,c,b]\)
几何应用 以\(\boldsymbol{a}, \boldsymbol{b}, \boldsymbol{c}\)为棱的平行六面体的体积：\(V = |[a,b,c]|\)
向量\(\boldsymbol{a}, \boldsymbol{b}, \boldsymbol{c}\)共面：\([a,b,c] = 0\)
